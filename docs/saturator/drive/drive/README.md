# Crucible (saturator) › Drive › Drive

[Back to Drive](../README.md) · [Crucible (saturator)](../../README.md)

How hard the signal is pushed into the saturation stage; more drive adds harmonics and density.

Part of **Crucible (saturator)** – Tube (12AX7, 12AT7, 12AU7, 6SN7, EF86, EL34, 6L6, KT88, 300B models with bias, blocking and sag), tape, soft/hard clip, foldback and asymmetric waveshaping with tone and parallel mix, plus three parallel tube ingredients to blend into the brew: a warm 6SN7, a hot 12AX7 and a crisp EF86 pentode.

**Range:** 0.00 dB … 36.0 dB (default: 6.00 dB)

Right-click the control for its menu: type a value, **Reset to default**, **Copy value** / **Paste value** (between any two knobs; a value of another unit lands at the same position on the scale), MIDI learn, an automation lane, and **Help**, which opens this page.

Drag up / down to turn it (Shift: fine), turn the mouse wheel over it, double-click or Ctrl+click to reset.
