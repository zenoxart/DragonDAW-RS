# Jadefang (character EQ) › Controls › Air freq

[Back to Controls](../README.md) · [Jadefang (character EQ)](../../README.md)

The frequency the effect works around. Drag it to move the effect through the spectrum.

Part of **Jadefang (character EQ)** – Six voicings of a four-band EQ with a driven input stage, a very broad Sub band and a boost-only Air band.

**Range:** Options: 2.5 kHz, 5 kHz, 10 kHz, 20 kHz, 40 kHz (default: 20 kHz)

## Mathematics

Air shelf frequency: 2.5, 5, 10, 20 or 40 kHz; frequencies above $0.45 f_s$ are approximated by a lower shelf
with reduced gain so the audible part of the curve matches.

**Knob position → value.** A choice of 5: the value is the option's index $0 \dots 4$.

**Smoothing.** Takes effect at once (no glide).

Right-click the control for its menu: **Set value…** (a box with the current value: type a new one in the control's units – `-6 dB`, `250 ms`, `2.5k`, `30L` – and press Enter; one undo step), **Reset to default**, **Copy value** / **Paste value** (between any two knobs; a value of another unit lands at the same position on the scale), MIDI learn, an automation lane, and **Help**, which opens this page.

Drag up / down to turn it (Shift: fine), turn the mouse wheel over it, double-click or Ctrl+click to reset.
